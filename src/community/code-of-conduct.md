# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in the Puddi project, in its repositories, issues, pull requests and any other project space, is expected to follow the [code of conduct](https://github.com/puddi-lang/puddi/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, give and accept technical criticism graciously, and do not harass anyone. The full text explains what is not acceptable, how to report a problem privately and how reports are handled.
